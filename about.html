Behind the Finish Line

Discover the history, the dedicated community, and the passion that drives every stride at the Marathon & Race Event.

EST. 2008

It Began with a
Single Morning Run

What started eighteen years ago with just 150 local community runners has evolved into an internationally recognized Abbott-qualifying marathon event hosting over 15,000 runners from across the globe.

"We didn't just organize a race; we built an unstoppable movement of health, endurance, and human triumph." - The Race Directors

Meet the Pacers & Crew

Our championship event is powered by over 2,500 dedicated volunteers, 80 certified pace leaders, and an expert medical team stationed along every kilometer to ensure runner safety, hydration, and peak motivation.

2,500+ Race Crew

A Vision for Sustainable Racing

We are committed to eco-friendly, zero-waste race operations: 100% biodegradable water cups, solar-powered timing mats, recycled metal finisher medals, and tree-planting initiatives for every registered runner.
